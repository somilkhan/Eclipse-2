This repository, [somilkhan/InstaEclipse](https://github.com/somilkhan/InstaEclipse), continues
InstaEclipse from the original codebase with the original history preserved. All credit for the
project's design and most of its features goes to ReSo7200 and the contributors listed in the
companion app and on the
[contributors graph](https://github.com/somilkhan/InstaEclipse/graphs/contributors).

## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Keep the supported Instagram version current (`TESTED_INSTAGRAM` in `ExportBrand.java`,
  README "Compatibility", `version.json`).
- Cut releases by pushing a `vX.Y.Z` tag (see [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md)).
- Hold the release signing key. It is stored only as GitHub Actions secrets and in the
  maintainer's offline backup; it is never committed.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors who have landed several good pull requests and help with triage may be
invited. Open a discussion or ask in the Telegram group.
